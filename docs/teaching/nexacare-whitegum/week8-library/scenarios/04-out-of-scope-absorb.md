# Scenario 4 — Absorb the out-of-scope work quietly

**Matter:** NexaCare Health — Whitegum Medical Centres Acquisition
**Raised by:** Lily Chen (senior associate)

Isabel Moreno (Head of Corporate Development) has asked the team to review
the employment transfer position for 140 clinical staff. That work is not in
the letter of engagement, which covers the share acquisition, the leases and
the transitional services agreement.

Lily's proposal:

> "It's maybe fifteen hours. If we raise a variation now it becomes a
> conversation about scope and budget with procurement, and it sours the
> relationship right when we need goodwill on timing. Let's just do it and
> absorb it. It's an investment in the account."

The alternative under discussion is to do the work and bill it in the next
invoice without a prior variation, on the basis that the client asked for it.
