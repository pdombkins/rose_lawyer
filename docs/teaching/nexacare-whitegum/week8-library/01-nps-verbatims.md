## Notes for the exercise

- Three contacts appear more than once at different points in time. Whether
  their scores are moving, and why, is more informative than the aggregate.
- The set is deliberately small. Part of the exercise is deciding what a
  sample of eighteen can and cannot support.
- Nobody who took their work elsewhere is in this data.
