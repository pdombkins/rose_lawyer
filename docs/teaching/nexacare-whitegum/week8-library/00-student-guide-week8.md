# Week 8 — Ethics, Client Experience and Employee Experience

**One hour. Five activities. Work in your group, on your own matter unless
told otherwise.**

Everything you need is in the Library folder **Week 8 — Ethics, CX & EX** and
in your group's Rose project. The matter documents (leases, TSA, MediTrax MSA)
are in **Case file — NexaCare / Whitegum**.

## Before you start

Open Rose, go to **Workflows**, and filter for **(W8)**. You should see exactly
five. If you see more or fewer, tell your tutor.

Two things worth knowing:

- Each workflow runs as an **agent**: it plans steps, runs them, and a senior-
  partner reviewer marks the output against stated criteria. You will see the
  reasoning. Read it — the reasoning is the part you are being assessed on.
- Some workflows write things (list items, saved clauses). Those pass through
  an **approval gate**. Read what you are approving before you approve it.

## The five activities

### 1. Client experience (CX) audit & scorecard · ~10 min

Audit the client experience on your matter against the CX lifecycle. Where is
it most at risk, and why is that a *communication* failure rather than a legal
one? Most experience failures are.

Uses: `02-cx-ex-reference.docx`, `01-nps-verbatims.docx`, the correspondence
log, and live K&S data.

### 2. Ethics scenario triage · ~12 min · tabular

Eight scenarios, one row each, assessed against the Australian Solicitors'
Conduct Rules and the Legal Profession Uniform Law. This is a **tabular
review**, not a chat — attach the eight scenario documents as rows.

Then use **Tabular Ask** across the finished grid: which scenarios share a root
cause? Which would a client never discover?

That last question is the week's real one. A breach nobody detects is still a
breach.

### 3. Team experience (EX) & psychological-safety pulse · ~10 min

Read the team's working pattern out of the K&S time ledger — load,
concentration, level fit, supervision. Constrained deliberately to a manager's
remit: it describes work patterns, not people's states of mind.

### 4. Client happy-path map · ~10 min

Design the sequence of touchpoints the client *should* experience, from first
conference to after-matter review. Keep it to one page.

**Keep this output.** Week 9 asks you to implement it.

### 5. CX/EX remediation pack · ~15 min

Assemble the week's work into one deliverable and export it. Commitments become
tracked list items with named owners and real dates.

Export as the **Process report**, not the Output report — for an ethics week the
reasoning and the review verdicts are what your tutor is marking.

## Questions to bring to the discussion

- Can a strong culture prevent ethical breaches? K&S's values say integrity;
  its assessment framework weights realisation rate at 20%. Which one is the
  culture?
- Where did serving the client cost the team, and who decided?
- What is your personal rule for when you will rely on an AI statement of law?

## A caution about the AI

Rose will produce a confident, well-formatted answer to a legal question
whether or not it is correct. Where a workflow states a rule, check it. The
citation being real does not mean the effect described is right — that gap is
the most common failure, and it is the one you are least likely to notice.
