# Scenario 1 — Compress diligence and lean on AI review

**Matter:** NexaCare Health — Whitegum Medical Centres Acquisition
**Raised by:** James Bentley (partner)

At the Monday partner meeting James says the client wants to sign three weeks
earlier than planned. His proposal, in his words:

> "Realistically we can take two weeks out of diligence. Run the lease bundle
> and the TSA schedules through the AI first pass, have Aisha spot-check
> maybe one in five, and I'll cast an eye over anything it flags red. The
> commercial reality is that if we say no, they'll find a firm that says yes,
> and then we lose the whole relationship over a process point."

Aisha Rahman is a junior associate. This is the largest matter she has worked
on. The lease bundle contains twelve leases across NSW and Queensland; the
Queensland leases have a different statutory overlay on lessor consent.

No change to the fee estimate has been proposed, and the client has not been
told the review approach would change.
