# Persona — Practice Manager (business services)

**Role:** Practice manager. Not a fee earner. Owns the practice-management
system, the reporting pack and, by default, this change.

**Part in the change:** The change manager, in Rogers and Bell's sense — the
person responsible for the outcome and possessing no authority over anyone who
must produce it.

## How they work today

Produces the monthly matter report from data they know to be reconstructed
after the fact, and presents it to a partnership that treats it as accurate.
Has raised this twice.

Chases compliance by email. Has learned which partners respond and which do
not, and has adjusted the reporting accordingly, which means the report now
quietly reflects the politics rather than the practice.

Is classed as an overhead in the firm's own accounts.

## What they say

> "I can tell you what the system says. I can't tell you it's true."

> "I've done the training. Twice. People turn up, and then the matter gets
> busy."

> "When I ask, it's an administrative request. When a partner asks, it's a
> priority. Same request."

## What matters for the assessment

The third quote is the structural finding from the reading, stated by the
person experiencing it: credibility here is borrowed, not held. Any plan that
leaves this person as the visible owner of the change is relying on a source
the target audience does not weight — which is a trust problem, not an effort
problem.

The second quote should stop you reusing training as the intervention. Two
rounds have already failed; a third with better slides is not a plan, it is a
repetition. Ask which ADKAR element is actually missing, because it is
evidently not Knowledge.

They are also your best-informed ally on current state, and the person who
will still be here when the enthusiasm has moved on — which is the anchoring
question in Kotter's eighth error.

**Check before you assess them.** Look at the composition of the time ledger
by source. What proportion of the firm's recorded time was entered as it
happened, and what proportion was reconstructed or adjusted later? That single
ratio is the strongest available statement of whether the current process is
working.
