# Scenario 7 — The checklist said it was fine

**Matter:** Group F — TechFlow Solutions, Employment Contract Review
**Raised by:** Quality review

The firm has standardised employment contract review: a fixed checklist, a
precedent clause bank, and an AI first pass that scores each contract against
the checklist and flags deviations.

A reviewer approved forty contracts in a day. One contained a restraint clause
that was unusual in its geographic scope. It was not on the checklist, so the
AI did not flag it and the reviewer did not raise it.

When asked, the reviewer said:

> "It came back clean. The checklist covers what we've agreed matters, and I
> got through the volume they asked for. If we're supposed to read every
> contract properly, then the whole point of standardising it disappears."

The client's position is that they instructed a review of the contracts, not a
review against a checklist.
