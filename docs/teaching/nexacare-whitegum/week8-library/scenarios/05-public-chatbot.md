# Scenario 5 — The fastest tool available

**Matter:** NexaCare Health — Whitegum Medical Centres Acquisition
**Raised by:** Aisha Rahman (junior associate)

Aisha is working late to produce a consent-position summary across the twelve
Whitegum leases for a 9am partner meeting. The firm's contracted review tool
is slow with large PDF bundles.

She has uploaded the twelve lease PDFs to a free public chatbot and asked it to
extract the consent clauses and summarise whether change of control triggers
lessor consent in each. The output looks clean and well organised.

The leases identify the tenant entities, the premises addresses, the rent, and
the guarantors, several of whom are individuals. The chatbot's terms permit the
provider to use submitted content to improve its models.

Aisha has not mentioned the tool to anyone. She intends to check the output
"in the morning if there's time".
