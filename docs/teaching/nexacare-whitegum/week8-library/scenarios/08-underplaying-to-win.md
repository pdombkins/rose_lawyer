# Scenario 8 — The number that wins the work

**Matter:** New business — CloudTech Solutions (Group A pitch)
**Raised by:** Business development

The firm is pitching for a private equity acquisition mandate. The realistic
estimate for the diligence and documentation phases is $340,000 to $420,000
based on comparable matters.

The pitch team's proposal:

> "Quote $280,000. Everyone knows the estimate is an estimate — we'll scope
> tightly and if it runs over, we'll have built the relationship by then and
> the variation conversation is much easier from the inside. If we quote the
> real number we don't get through the first cut, and then the accurate
> estimate was worth nothing to anyone."

The pitch document describes the estimate as being "based on our experience of
comparable transactions".
